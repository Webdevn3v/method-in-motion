Our Story

Welcome to
Method in Motion

Method in Motion isn't just another coding website. It's an entire universe built to help kids become creators instead of consumers. Around here, every lesson is an adventure, every mistake is part of the story, and every bug is simply another clue waiting to be solved.

Create. Don't Consume.
Our Philosophy

A Different Way to Learn

Most coding platforms teach programming like another school subject. We believe kids learn best when they're having fun.

Instead of memorizing endless syntax, Method in Motion teaches coding through characters, stories, games, music, comics, challenges, creativity, and exploration.

Children don't just read about coding. They experience it. They build it. They remember it.

The Universe

Welcome to the Universe

The Method in Motion Universe is made up of connected worlds where every adventure teaches a real programming skill.

MiM University

This is where new creators begin. Inside MiM University you'll meet our coding crew, discover new ideas, learn programming concepts, unlock printable activities, and build confidence one lesson at a time.

MiM Underground

Hidden beneath the university is MiM Underground — the creative maker space where imagination comes to life. Here you'll find games, experiments, projects, hidden surprises, and opportunities to put everything you've learned into action.

Learning doesn't stop here. This is where creating begins.

The Origin

Built with Heart

Method in Motion started with one laptop, one big dream, and the imagination of a mom and her young daughter.

Rather than waiting until everything was perfect, we decided to start building. You'll probably discover a few glitches along the way — and that's okay. Every update makes this universe better.

Found something? If you find a typo, bug, broken link, or have an idea for something fun, we'd love to hear from you. You're helping build Method in Motion alongside us.
