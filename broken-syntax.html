Meet the Band

Who's Behind the Noise

Tap a band member to read their interview — straight from the MiM Underground press room.

Lead Vocals & Guitar
Bug
"Every bug is just a clue — turns out that works for lyrics too."
Q: What's "HTML (How NOT To Make Lasagna)" actually about?
Nesting things wrong. You stack layer after layer and forget to close one, and suddenly the whole dish collapses. Same thing happens with tags. I wrote it after a very bad dinner and a worse bug.
Q: Loudest part of being in this band?
Me. Obviously. Someone has to be the alarm going off when things break — might as well make it sound good.
Q: Any advice for someone starting their first song, or their first line of code?
Make the mess first. You can't fix something that doesn't exist yet.
Drums
Zen
"If you can imagine the beat, you can play it."
Q: Why drums, out of everything?
Rhythm is just structure you can feel. I spend all day thinking about layout and spacing — drums are the same idea, just louder and way more fun.
Q: What's it like writing with Bug and Loop?
Bug brings the chaos, Loop keeps the count, and I just try to make it all look good on stage. It works better than it should.
Q: One thing you want people to take from the song?
Messy first drafts are allowed. The good version comes after, not instead of.
Bass
Loop
"Every pattern has a reason — including this bassline."
Q: How does bass connect to what you teach about loops?
A bassline is a loop you can dance to. Same start, same stop, same step, repeating until the song tells it otherwise.
Q: What's your role in the band, really?
I hold the pattern steady so Bug can be loud and Zen can get creative on top of it. Someone has to keep the count.
Q: Favorite part of recording the single?
Watching Bug try to explain lasagna nesting to a producer who had never made lasagna. Took four takes.
