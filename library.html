Did You Know?

Coding facts that'll blow your mind.

Real facts about coding, computers, and the internet — the kind of things that make you go "wait, what?"

🐛
The First Bug Was Real
In 1947, a moth was found stuck inside a computer at Harvard. It was literally a bug — and that's where the word came from.
🌐
The Web Is Only 35 Years Old
Tim Berners-Lee invented the World Wide Web in 1989. The internet feels ancient but it's actually newer than most of your teachers.
💾
Apollo Used Less Power Than Your Phone
The computer that sent humans to the moon had less processing power than a basic scientific calculator today.
🎮
The First Video Game Was in 1958
Tennis for Two was created on an oscilloscope by physicist William Higinbotham — decades before consoles existed.
👩‍💻
The First Programmer Was a Woman
Ada Lovelace wrote the first computer algorithm in 1843 — for a machine that hadn't even been built yet.
📱
There Are 5 Billion Websites
Every one of them started as a blank HTML file. Yours could be next.
History of Coding

How we got here.

From punch cards to Python — the story of how coding became the most important skill on the planet.

1843
Ada Lovelace Writes the First Algorithm
For Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine — a computer that existed only on paper. She saw what machines could do before machines could do it.
1940s
The First Electronic Computers
ENIAC filled an entire room. It could do 5,000 additions per second. Your phone does billions.
1969
ARPANET — The Internet Is Born
The US Defense Department connected four university computers. The first message sent was "lo" — the system crashed before "login" was complete.
1989
Tim Berners-Lee Invents the Web
The World Wide Web was originally a project to help scientists share research. It accidentally became everything.
1995
JavaScript Is Created in 10 Days
Brendan Eich wrote the first version of JavaScript in just 10 days. It now runs on 98% of all websites.
2024
AI Changes Everything (Again)
The coders who understand how things work will be the ones who build what comes next. That's you.
